The part that isn't a joke

Come be wrong with us in person.

We meet every week to talk about philosophy, rationality, AI, and whether a crab cake with more than 4% filler is still a crab cake (it isn't). Newcomers who have read zero blog posts are, in our estimation, roughly as welcome as those who have read all of them.

Frequently Asked Questions (by us, to ourselves)

FAQ

Is this real?

The meetup is real. This website is a joke. If something is inside a green “ACTUALLY REAL INFORMATION” box, it was copied from public listings. Everything else should get a credence somewhere between “satire” and “a bit.”

Is this the official site?

No. It's a parody fan page. For anything official, email the organizer at rivka@adrusi.com or check the LessWrong group page.

Why “Defwrong”?

We did a cost-benefit analysis on becoming less wrong and found the marginal returns were declining. Being definitely wrong, by contrast, is cheap, reliable and scalable. We are also, somewhere in our hearts, still trying to be less wrong. Don't tell anyone.

Is the meetup actually in Baltimore?

Here is a fact: UMBC stands for University of Maryland, Baltimore County, which is a separate jurisdiction from Baltimore City. Its mailing address says “Baltimore” and the campus is near Catonsville and Arbutus. So we're wrong about our own name before the meetup even starts. Efficient.

Do I need to have read the Sequences?

No. Nobody checks. Half of us have read them, a quarter claim to have read them, and the remaining quarter are still on “The Simple Truth.” You're welcome to just show up and talk.

Will AI kill us all?

Great question for the meetup. Allow three hours. Bring snacks.

Where do I park?

For real: parking at UMBC is free on weekends, and the meetup is on Sunday. Our model rarely gets anything this right.

Why is there a mouse playing a lute?

Medieval monks filled the margins of serious manuscripts with knights fighting snails, rabbits holding swords, and animals playing instruments. We think this is the correct way to annotate a serious text. The mouse is our marginalia. Its credence that it is a good lute player is 0.97, which is not well calibrated. We use it a lot.

Is Mr. Trash Wheel an aligned AI?

He eats trash at the mouth of the Jones Falls, has googly eyes, and has never once tried to seize resources beyond the Inner Harbor. Professor Trash Wheel, Captain Trash Wheel and Gwynnda the Good Wheel of the West also seem fine. In terms of track record, they are the most aligned systems in Maryland.
